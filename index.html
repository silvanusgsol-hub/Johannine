The activity types

  • 🔍 Narrative Detective: read a clue and identify the point it proves.
  • ⇅ Story Sequencer: put events, scenes or sayings in order.
  • ⇄ Theme & Character Matcher: pair characters, sayings and meanings.
  • 🗂 Arrange the Evidence: sort clues into categories.
  • 🛤 Choose Your Path: respond to a church situation and see the consequence.
  • 📜 Unlock the Scroll: type the missing word; close variants are accepted.
  • 🏛 Build the Story: rebuild an outline, chiasm or map slot by slot.
  • ✍ Reflection Challenge: write your answer, then compare it with a model.
